The Workshop

This workshop offers a unique opportunity to acquire comprehensive knowledge and strengthen skills in the field of designing electric vehicles. It provides a forum for hands-on training on fundamental aspects of vehicle dynamics, components, selection, and design both theoretically and practically.

MIT Mechanical

Equipped with labs in collaboration with Bosch Rexroth, ABB, IIT Bombay, and Beckhoff Automation. The department offers UG (Mechatronics), PG (Industrial Automation), and Ph.D. programs.

CSRM & SEVC

The Solar Electric Vehicle Championship (SEVC) is conducted by CSRM in collaboration with HIPDS and SSTMH. This workshop is specifically designed for teams targeting competitions like BAJA, QUADBIKE, and SUPRA.

Curriculum & Topics

01 Vehicle design overview & Project planning
02 Solar powered Electric Autonomous Vehicles
03 Suspension Design, Analysis & Calculation
04 Transmission system selection & design
05 Motor & Controller - Calculation & Selection
06 Electric two-wheeler design concepts
07 Brakes - Foundation concepts & design
08 Battery Management Systems (Live Demo)
09 Steering Design & Calculations (Live Demo)
10 Design and Modelling of Roll cage
11 Roll cage Simulation (Hands-on Training)
12 Vehicle CAD assembly & Solar Systems
